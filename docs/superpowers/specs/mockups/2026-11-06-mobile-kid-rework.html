1 · Parent onboarding

Account creation moves into the phone (today it's web only). Seven short steps, each one screen. The ideas are taught with three swipe cards before any form, then each step says why in one line.

2 · Child onboarding: learn by playing

About 2 minutes. Each idea is one screen where the child does the thing with practice coins (marked PRACTICE, never touches real jars). Ends with a goal and a Jarso that's theirs. Skippable, replayable from Me.

3 · Child home and the + button

Tabs: Home · Learn · + · Diary · Jarso. The + is the one way to record money. Bell and avatar sit on top. Home has 4 blocks, all big.

4 · Money flows: one question per screen

Every flow: how much (big keypad) → what for (picture grid) → celebration. Rules and limits show as one chip, not sentences. The "guess what happens to your goal" step becomes a quick Jarso question on the amount screen.

5 · Jars and goal

The line graph is gone. Tap a jar tile → its page with a big drawn jar and 6 weekly bars with the number on each bar. The goal is a big picture with a ring and milestone stars.

6 · Learn: a quest map

Missions become stops on a winding path, one week per stretch, with a chest at the end. Questions get big picture answers and Jarso reacts. A daily 1-tap check-in keeps the diary honest and the streak alive.

7 · Diary, Jarso, Me, bell

Same features, fewer words, bigger icons. Notifications move from a tab to the bell. Me becomes the trophy room.

8 · Parent app (same system, calmer)

Parents get the new tokens, keycaps and Jarso, but denser and quieter: no Sparks, no confetti. What needs them is always on top, answerable in one tap.
