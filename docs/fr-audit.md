# Functional requirements audit

Every functional requirement in `REQUIREMENTS.md`, and the first-milestone spec (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-28-first-milestone-design.md`), checked against `main` and the hosted database. Verified from the source tree, migrations, tests, CI runs and the hosted catalog, not from PR descriptions. Audited 2026-10-31 and refreshed from the code on 2026-11-06.

Legend: **Built** = on `main`, usable end to end, tested, live on production. Numbers after `#` are pull requests. "On the phone" means the Expo app in `apps/mobile` does it too.

App install: `/manifest.webmanifest` with the brand icons and `apple-mobile-web-app` metadata. Everything works with the VAPID keys unset. Optional Resend email adapter (invitation email only). Due badges and the parent's "Needs you" list are on screen but are not notifications. On the phone: a Notifications tab for the child and the parent, live, with the same notices and an unread badge (#94, #98). | 5 |

## Beyond the requirements

Built on `main` although no FR asked for it.

- **Parent family dashboard** (#78, #85, #115, #116, #121, #126): Home leads with the lesson road, each child standing on their mission as one of the four coach kids, then the family's jars beside the goal track, what needs the parent and Jarso's summary; Children has one card per child with Learning and Money as separate halves; Money has the family chart, Worth knowing, spending by category and what is coming up, with a child filter and a week, month or 3-month switch.
- **Jarso's family summary** (#85, #116): a few sentences about the children's learning this week from Claude Haiku, built from the app's own figures with no text a child typed, shown only when every number in it checks out, and labelled as written by AI. The phone's Family home shows it too (#98).
- **Phone app** (#94, #98, #101, #102, #129, #141, #145, #146, #147; ADR 0015 to 0018): an Expo app for children and parents on the same Supabase backend, with the web's copy and rules shared through `@jarso/core` and `@jarso/data`, an offline outbox, native push, native payment sheets, short settings menus and Android builds with FCM (#108, #114, #123).
- **Characters** (#71, #77, #80, #85, #141): four coach kids, Bo, Zuzu, Pip and Nell, across the child's screens and lessons; Jarso drawn as the logo jar with eyes; on the phone, Jarso as an animated jar with moods that speaks the coaching lines and cheers in celebrations.
- **Sounds** (#80, #107, #141): coins that clink, a chime or a soft bonk on answers, a mission fanfare and Jarso's clink, synthesised in the browser behind one Sounds switch; short clips with their own switch on the phone.
- **Celebrations** (#119, #141): a success dialog for a new gift, a new allowance, matched saving, an approved request, a confirmed hand-over and a new goal; full-screen celebrations with a sound and a haptic on the phone.
- **Welcome tour** (#138): on a child's first visit the screen dims, one thing glows, and the buddy the child picks walks them through each tab in a recorded or device voice; it shows once per child on any device and replays from the child's settings.
- **Voice** (#85, #110, #122, #139): Read aloud on lesson steps, lesson films with a recorded voice, and a microphone for asking Jarso.
- **Progress engine** (#143): Sparks, 10 levels, 23 badges, a forgiving streak, a daily check-in and the child's Jarso look, awarded inside the database by triggers on the ledger, lessons and goals. Sparks are never money.
- **Child-first design** (#54, #55, #62, #90, #125, #140): plain words and a text floor, money as cards and sentences rather than tables, a desktop and laptop layout with a left rail, an audit pass for copy, dark mode and tap sizes, and a child Home with fewer words.
