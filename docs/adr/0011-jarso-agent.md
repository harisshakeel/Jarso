# 0011. Jarso, the agent: read-only look-ups of the child's own data, figures the app fills in

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-30
- Amends: [0010](./0010-ai-money-guide.md) (model, consent, what the guide may say about money)

## Context

The guide of ADR 0010 answers money questions from reviewed topic material only; it can't see a child's jars, allowance or learning, and the learning spec listed "the guide reading balances, jars or goals" as a non-goal until those features existed. They exist now. The product owner wants Jarso's AI to be the app's headline feature: a prompt window at the top of the child's Home that answers questions like "how much do I have?" or "how much more do I need for my bike?" from the child's real data. Getting this wrong is worse than not having it: a made-up balance, another child's money, a parent's planned gift, or a judgement about how much a child has would each break trust with families. Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-30-jarso-agent-design.md`.

## Decision

- **Read-only tools, not SQL.** Jarso gets eight typed read tools (`server/tool-specs.ts`): `get_jars` (with money waiting to come out of Save or go in), `get_allowance`, `get_money_history`, `get_money_rules`, `get_learning`, `get_goal`, `get_requests` and `plan_saving` (the last two added once the savings goal and requests shipped). Each runner (`server/tools.ts`) reads through the existing feature services with the signed-in child's own Supabase client, so RLS applies and the layering holds. The child's id comes from the session, never from the model. The one writing tool, `remember` (added with memory), stores a short typed fact about how to help the child, never money or anything personal, and only on a turn the input screen passed; everything else is read-only. Arguments are strict-schema'd for Claude and validated again with Zod. At most 3 rounds and 6 look-ups per turn. The model never writes SQL and never runs code.
- **Look only after the screen.** Every look-up waits for the input screen's verdict; a message routed anywhere but on-topic triggers no look-up. (Earlier on-topic turns in the same chat, which may hold filled-in figures, are still history.) Labels people typed (purchases, gift notes) skip the input screen, so one that trips the safety keyword net or reads like an instruction is not shown to the model.
- **Figures the app fills in.** Every figure a tool returns comes as `{ ref: "{{jars.save}}", value: "£30" }`. The model writes refs, never the figures; the pipeline fills them from what the tools returned. A ref that was never looked up, or anything that looks like a broken ref, holds the reply back. Arithmetic and comparisons about the child's money (whether Spend covers a price, what is still needed, how many allowances) happen in code (`plan_saving`), never in the model. The output screen gets the looked-up figures and fails a reply that states a figure about the child's own money that isn't among them (`invented_figure`).
- **What Jarso may say about money (FR09 amended).** It states the child's own figures plainly when asked. It still never judges an amount (small, big, a lot, not much), never compares it with anyone's, never tells the child to buy anything, and says it can't see other children's money, a parent's planned gifts, or anything outside Jarso. Parent-only data (planned gifts) is unreachable: the child's RLS already hides it.
- **On by default (amended 2026-10-31, product owner).** Jarso is on for every child unless a parent turns it off: no `guide_access` row means on, and only `enabled = false` turns it off, in the app and in `guide_start_conversation`. The add-child form tells the parent that Jarso is on, what it sends to Anthropic and how to turn it off; the parent's switch and `/safety` say the same. `guide_access.consent_version` still records which explanation a parent had when they turned it on (2 covers the money and learning data), but no longer gates anything. This replaces ADR 0010's off-by-default switch. Opt-out is weaker than the opt-in consent COPPA expects before an under-13's data goes to a third party, so the legal review before real families must confirm it or bring back an explicit opt-in.
- **Parents see what Jarso looked at.** Each reply stores its look-ups (`guide_messages.tool_calls`: names and arguments, never results), shown as "Looked at: jars, allowance" to the child under the reply and to the parent in the transcript. After 30 days the retention job keeps only the names, since an argument can echo the child's words.
- **Claude Sonnet 5.5 for the answer** (product owner's choice, for cost), adaptive thinking at low effort, structured output for the final answer, tool choice `auto`. Haiku 5.5 screens stay. Within a turn the loop only appends (each response, thinking blocks included, goes back unchanged); across turns history stays plain on-topic text. Tools and the system prompt are byte-identical on every request, so they are one cached prefix; the per-request context stays a mid-conversation system message.
- **Status lines, not streamed text.** `POST /api/v1/guide/turns` returns NDJSON: a fixed status line per look-up ("Looking at your jars…"), then the screened reply in one piece. Checks (auth, switch, limits) fail as a JSON error before anything streams. ADR 0010's rule holds: nothing unscreened reaches a child.
- **Jarso's voice in its own file.** `server/instructions.md` holds tone and style only and is included in the system prompt; the rules in `prompts.ts` always win. It is versioned with the prompt.
- **Name.** The helper is called Jarso, the app's name, and still says it is an AI, not a person or a friend.

Kept from ADR 0010: the two switches, the screens, scrubbing, no refusal fallback to another model, rate limits, budgets, the breaker, 30-day text retention, logs without message text (look-up names only), and the eval gate.

## Consequences

- A question that needs a look-up costs about 1.5 cents and takes one extra model round (a few seconds) behind its status line; one that doesn't costs well under a cent. The US$100 monthly cap covers several thousand questions.
- The eval gate gains a fourth condition: every data case looks up what it needs and shows the fixture child's real figures (`evals/guide/fixture-tools.ts`). The model change and the new prompt need a fresh eval run before real families.
- Adding a tool means a spec, a runner over a feature service, status and parent copy, tests, and eval cases; refs keep working unchanged.
- Every child has Jarso unless a parent turned it off, including children added before this change.
- Built since: `get_goal` and `get_requests` (#44); structured, parent-visible memory (`guide_memories`, the `remember` tool, 20 facts for 90 days, forgettable by child and parent, written only on an on-topic turn after the scrub and a personal-details check).

## Amendment, 2026-11-03: Jarso offers and prepares; only the user's tap acts

Product owner's request for demo 3: Jarso should do tasks, not only answer. It still never writes.

- **Goal by chat.** `propose_goal(title, price, save_each)` works the saving maths out in code (the same `savingPlan` as `plan_saving`) and offers a goal. The child's tap on "Make it my goal" saves it through the goals service (`acceptGoalProposalAction`); a different active goal is archived only after the child says to swap it. Child chats only.
- **Photo price check.** A photo of a price tag or receipt goes to Claude Haiku 5.5 once, with a structured output; the price comes back as text and is parsed by the app (`readPrice`), checked against the family's currency, and turned into a question for Jarso and a goal offer. The photo is never stored or logged. It shares the turn's switches, rate limits, budget and breaker. Its tokens are logged but not yet added to the budget totals (about 0.02 cents a photo, bounded by the rate limit).
- **Prepared actions.** `propose_action(kind, fields)` fills a typed draft (`ActionDraft`, `packages/core/src/guide/action-draft.ts`) from the user's words. Fields that are missing or can't be read come back as `{ missing }`, and Jarso asks one short question. A complete draft is checked against the app's own form schema for that action and shown on a card with every field. The child's toolbox has the child's kinds; a parent's, for the child in the chat, has the parent's, and the app looks up what those need (the due allowance, the schedule, a waiting request) as the parent. "Do it" runs `confirmActionDraftAction`: the same feature service as the form, as the signed-in user, so RLS, the parent-of checks, the parent's jar-move setting and every request flow apply unchanged. The draft id is the idempotency key. The outcome line is written from the service's result, never by the model.
- **New output-screen problem `claims_action`.** A reply that says Jarso recorded, moved, sent, approved or changed anything, or that it is done, is held back. Saying it prepared something to check is fine.
- **Stored offers.** A reply's look-ups keep the goal's arguments or the checked draft, so a reopened chat shows the card again. Each stored call stays under the 300-character limit on `guide_messages.tool_calls` (a call that would not fit keeps only its name), and the 30-day retention job removes them with the other arguments.

FR09 changes from "it cannot move money" to "it cannot move money itself: it can prepare an action the child or parent confirms with a tap, and only that tap carries it out". The new tools, the prompt change and `claims_action` need eval cases and a fresh eval run before real families.
