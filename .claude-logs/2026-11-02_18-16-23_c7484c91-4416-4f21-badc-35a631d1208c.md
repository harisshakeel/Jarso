Here are the 16 functional requirements from [REQUIREMENTS.md](REQUIREMENTS.md), with ownership taken from the squash-merged PR authors recorded in [fr-audit.md](docs/fr-audit.md#L82-L104) and the GitHub PR list. All 16 are built on main.

**FR01 Allowance schedule and handover.** Pratyush, fixes by Haris.
- Weekly, monthly, quarterly or yearly schedule that falls due exactly once from an hourly job. Parent can change, pause, resume or stop it.
- Parent confirms handover on one screen with the Spend/Save split, starting from the last split. One-time gifts stay hidden from the child until handed over.
- Pratyush built it in PRs 18 and 19, plus card handover via Stripe and Razorpay test mode in 56. Haris fixed handover results, pause and resume in 23, 24 and 25.

**FR02 Personal savings goal.** Pratyush, with Haris for Jarso and prediction.
- One active goal per child with a name, emoji, price and a plan per allowance.
- Progress counts confirmed Save less reserved withdrawals, never due or pending money. Estimates payments left and updates as the plan changes.
- Pratyush built the goal in PR 41. Haris made Jarso read it in 44 and added the prediction step in 46.

**FR03 Spend and Save jars.** Pratyush, fixes by Haris.
- Spend is cash the child holds, Save is money the parent keeps. Each jar shows its confirmed balance with due money shown separately.
- Moves between jars are deposit or withdrawal requests, never new income. The child Home draws both as filling jars.
- Pratyush did the split at handover in 18, jar moves in 40 and the jar Home in 42. Haris fixed the split in 23. Child split planning was deferred by decision.

**FR04 Money diary.** Pratyush.
- Child records purchases with an amount, category chip and optional name, and "Got money" from other sources that counts at once.
- Entries can be fixed within 7 days or undone within 24 hours, kept as a traceable chain. The linked parent sees the diary and a "where your money went" card.
- Built in PRs 30 and 31, the Diary tab in 42, and completed with chips, money received and corrections in 65.

**FR05 Predict before deciding.** Haris.
- Before a move that touches savings, the child predicts whether the goal gets further, stays the same or gets closer.
- The app then reveals the real answer with a plain explanation and a what-if slider that moves no money.
- Wired into the take-out, put-in and change-plan sheets in PRs 46 and 48. A prediction before a purchase is still open.

**FR06 Interactive daily learning.** Haris.
- Seven-day journey of missions with 6 to 8 steps each: choices, amounts, a Spend/Save slider, sorting, matching, predictions and reasons.
- Instant feedback, two free hints, two tries, missed steps replayed at the end, and progress kept between visits.
- Spaced practice brings concepts back in new situations after 1, 3, 7 and 14 days. Built in PR 28, ADR 0009.

**FR07 AI money guide (Jarso).** Haris. Built behind a flag.
- Claude Sonnet answers with Haiku input and output screens. "Ask Jarso" sits at the top of the child's Home.
- Read-only look-ups of the child's own jars, allowance, history, rules, goal, requests and learning. Figures come from the app, never the model.
- Hint ladder, topic links, thumbs up or down, rate limits and budgets, plus a short-fact memory. PRs 29, 32, 38, 44, 47 and 70.

**FR08 Related learning material.** Haris.

- Opening a topic never changes progress. Built in PR 28, guide links in 29.

**FR09 Guide boundaries.** Haris. Built behind a flag.

**FR10 Learning progress.** Haris.

**FR11 Weekly review.** Haris.

**FR12 Return experience.** Haris.

**FR14 Purchases, limits, deposits and withdrawals.** Pratyush.

Close, but two corrections if you want it to be accurate.

**Guardrails are not one layer at the end.** They sit on both sides: the Haiku input screen runs before the model sees anything, and the output screen runs after. Nothing unscreened reaches the child. So the picture is screen → model → screen, not model → screen.

**Finance info and database access are the same layer.** The model never touches the database. It calls typed read-only tools, and each tool reads through the app's feature services with the child's own Supabase client, so row-level security applies. There is no separate "database layer" the agent can reach.

The honest five-layer version, in the order a message travels:

1. Input guardrail. Scrub plus the Haiku input screen.
2. Voice and rules. Jarso's tone in instructions.md, the FR09 rules in the prompt. Rules always win over tone.
3. Child context and memory. Who the child is, their learning state, and the short facts Jarso kept.
4. Finance tools. Read-only look-ups of jars, allowance, history, rules, goal and requests, behind row-level security, with the app filling in every figure.
5. Output guardrail. Haiku output screen, ref check, length check, then fixed copy if anything fails.

If you want to keep it to a single "guardrail" word for a slide, say "guardrails on both ends" rather than "guardrail last".

- Before any move that touches savings, the child guesses: does the goal get further, stay the same, or get closer?
- Then the real answer with a plain explanation, and a what-if slider that moves no money.

- Monday to Sunday: received, spent, moved into Save, still due, and the goal line.

- Ends with a fresh question on something already learned. Praises thinking, never amounts. Shows on Home Friday to Monday.
