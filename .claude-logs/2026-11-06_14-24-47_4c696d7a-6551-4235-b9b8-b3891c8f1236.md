### Voices (the biggest gap)
- **Only Nell has a real voice.** There are 9 clips in [apps/web/public/lessons/voice/](apps/web/public/lessons/voice/), made by [lesson-voice.py](scripts/lesson-voice.py) with OpenRouter's free Fish Audio model (`s2.1-pro-free`) in the "Megan" voice: soft, young, British. ### The Jarso agent
- Requests go to `POST /api/v1/guide/turns`, which streams status lines ("Getting that ready for you to check…") and then one reply that has already passed the safety screen.
- Claude Sonnet 5.5 writes the answer and Haiku 5.5 screens what comes in and goes out. It has read-only look-up tools plus `propose_action` and `propose_goal`. The app fills in every money figure itself.
- "I spent $5 on…" becomes a **purchase draft card** ("Check this, then tap Do it", then **Do it**). Only the child's tap records it.
- To get **real replies** without touching real children: either you send the two prompts from a demo child account and paste the replies, or I write a small capture script modelled on the eval fixture ([fixture-tools.ts](apps/web/evals/guide/fixture-tools.ts)) with trailer demo data. The script needs Node and your Anthropic key, so it needs your consent.
