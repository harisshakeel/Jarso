**Logos** are in `public/brand/`, the folder Next.js serves static files from: the jar mark as `jarso-mark.png` and the wordmark as `jarso-wordmark.png`. One caveat: the wordmark is not transparent. It has a near-white baked-in background, so it only works on white surfaces until the designer exports an SVG. The two files also differ slightly in colour. I based the palette on the mark, since it becomes the app icon a child sees every day, and noted both points as open items for the designer.

**The design system** is at `docs/design.md`, linked from the docs index, CLAUDE.md's reading list, and the UI skill, so any agent touching UI reads it first. It encodes all twelve of your answers. The highlights:

- **Colour.** Brand teal, gold and cyan measured from the pixels, expanded into 50 to 950 tonal scales. Primary is `teal-700` rather than the raw brand teal, because the brand value is only 4.95:1 on white while `teal-700` is 6.9:1 both as text and under white button text. Gold is a fill colour with ink text, never text on white, with `gold-600` as its text form. Dark mode flips primary to `teal-400` and lets brand gold become a text colour at 10.5:1. Every ratio in the document is measured, not guessed.
- **Money and status.** Teal for Spend, gold for Save and goals, cyan for "due", dashed gold for "reserved", neutral for pending and rejected. Red never touches an amount, so a child is never shamed by colour.
- **Type.** Nunito throughout, with a money scale in tabular numerals so amounts line up. A one-time check that Nunito's tabular figures align is called out.
- **Shape and layout.** 14 px control radius giving roughly 20 px cards, 4 px spacing grid, 360 px first, bottom tab bar with child and parent tab sets, 44 px targets.
- **Components, motion, voice, role emphasis, accessibility** each have a section, and section 13 is a ready-to-paste `globals.css` token block for light and dark.
